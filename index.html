PhD candidate, UIUC · LLMs · Post-training · Optimization · Multi-turn Decision Making

I was the first to discover emergent structures in on-policy training, which later spawned a research thread at the intersection of optimization and on-policy training in LLMs. I work on Reinforcement Learning for LM post-training with Dilek Hakkani-Tür and Hao Peng. Lately I’ve been thinking about agentic sequential decision making — specifically, the delayed consequences of actions and partial observability. Research Directions

Optimization in Post-Training

How does on-policy data shape the optimization landscape and training dynamics of language models? I study the structure that emerges during on-policy training—from sparse parameter updates to preserved spectral structure—and use these insights to develop more efficient methods for LLM post-training.

  • Reinforcement Learning Finetunes Small Subnetworks in Large Language Models (NeurIPS 2025)
  • Do We Need Adam? Surprisingly Strong and Sparse Reinforcement Learning with SGD in LLMs (ICML 2026 · Oral)
  • ISO: An RLVR-Native Optimization Stack (Preprint)
  • Understanding LLM Parameter Update Sparsity through the Lens of Fisher (Preprint)

Sequential Decision Making

How does the notion of “Delayed consequences of actions” and “Partial Observability” affect reasoning and decision making in LLMs?

Work in progress — first results soon.
